SIGAP

Calibrated TB screening triage · UnivaBio 2026 · AI for Human Health · github.com/Alvnvnc/loop-tb

Problem

Indonesia carries 10% of the world’s TB burden (WHO Global TB Report 2025). About 200,000 people per year transmit TB before ever being detected, and screening is bottlenecked by chest-X-ray reading capacity at the last mile. Root cause: the care loop moves slower than transmission.

What we built — one calibrated risk state, three interfaces

1 · Screen. A chest X-ray becomes a calibrated risk with a deferral band: when model families disagree, SIGAP says “uncertain — human read” instead of forcing a verdict.

2 · Refer. The score maps to an action band (refer today / re-read / educate) and a printable referral letter for GeneXpert confirmation.

3 · Support. The same risk state follows the patient home: adherence check-ins update the score and trigger escalation.

Demo: two real external cases

REFER TB+ X-ray → p(TB)=0.98, disagreement u=0.01

UNCERTAIN normal X-ray from an unseen site → p(TB)=0.32, u=0.37 → “human re-read”

English + Indonesian UI; web-first responsive (desktop screening workspace, mobile parity) for community health workers; <3 minutes per patient; audit-friendly outputs.

Representation study (3 families). Domain-pretrained SSL (RAD-DINO, chest X-ray) beats supervised fine-tuning by +0.21 AUROC on unseen hospitals, while stacking/ensembling across families hurts (tested, reported).

Honesty & limitations

  • Research triage prototype — not a diagnostic device; GeneXpert confirmation stays mandatory.
  • WHO target (sens > 90%) is not yet met; reported as-is (0.71 at spec 0.90).
  • No Indonesian data in training; site thresholds do not fully transfer — measured over-triage is reported, not hidden.
  • Retrospective public data (radiographic labels), aggressive re-processing may evade de-duplication.
